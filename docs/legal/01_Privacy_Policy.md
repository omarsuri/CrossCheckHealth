## 3. Categories of Personal Data Collected

3.1 **Identification and contact data:** name, email address, mobile number, password (stored as a salted hash), date of birth.

3.2 **Health-related data ("Health Data"):** age, sex, height, weight, body measurements, lifestyle responses (diet, sleep, activity, smoking, alcohol), self-reported family history, self-reported symptoms, assessment responses across heart, body composition, BMI, and other awareness tools, and the outputs generated.

3.3 **Parent / Parivar Family Profile data:** identification, contact, and Health Data of the adult family member submitted by you, subject to independent consent from that family member (see Clause 6).

3.4 **Transaction data:** subscription plan selected, payment status, invoice records, masked instrument metadata. We do **not** store full card or bank credentials; payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant gateways.

3.5 **Device and usage data:** IP address, device identifier, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, clickstream, and cookie identifiers.

3.6 **User-generated content:** support messages, feedback, reviews, comments, images of product labels uploaded to the scanner.

3.7 **Communications data:** records of WhatsApp / SMS / email exchanges initiated by you with our support or grievance teams.

3.8 The Platform is intended for users aged **18 years and above**. We do **not** knowingly collect personal data of children below 18 years. If we discover such collection, the data will be deleted and the account will be terminated.

## 4. Purposes of Processing and Legal Basis

4.1 We process personal data only for the following specified, lawful purposes (Section 4, DPDP Act):

(a) Account creation, authentication, and Platform access;
(b) Generating personalised health-awareness insights, risk profiles, and reports;
(c) Operating the ingredient/product scanner and product comparison tools;
(d) Operating Parent and Family profiles (Parivar Health);
(e) Subscription management, payment processing, and invoicing;
(f) Customer support and grievance redressal;
(g) Product improvement, algorithm validation, and research, using **de-identified or aggregated data only**;
(h) Service-related communication (e.g., feature updates, security advisories) and, with separate opt-in, marketing communications;
(i) Compliance with applicable law, court orders, and regulatory directions;
(j) Detection, investigation, and prevention of fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

4.2 **Legal basis for processing.** All processing is based on the User's free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous **consent** obtained through an affirmative action (Section 6, DPDP Act), unless processing falls under "**legitimate uses**" enumerated in Section 7 of the DPDP Act (e.g., compliance with a court order, medical emergency, employment-related obligations).

4.3 **Consent withdrawal.** You may withdraw consent at any time through your account settings or by writing to admin@crosscheckhealth.in. Withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal. Withdrawal may, however, render certain Platform features unusable.

6.1 When you add a Parent or Family profile, you confirm and warrant that:

(a) The profile is of an **adult (≥18 years)** and is not a child or a person with a disability requiring a lawful guardian;
(b) You have a genuine relationship and a lawful basis to invite that person;
(c) You will share the verifiable consent link with that person; and
(d) You will not impersonate, coerce, or mislead the invitee.

6.2 **No Health Data is processed for a Parent or Family profile until the invitee has independently and affirmatively consented through the secure verification link sent by the Company.**

6.3 The invited family member is treated as an **independent Data Principal**. They may:

(a) access, correct, and erase their data;
(b) withdraw consent;
(c) make a grievance directly with the Company;
(d) raise a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India;
all by writing to admin@crosscheckhealth.in.

6.4 The Company maintains a verifiable consent ledger for each Parent and Family profile.

10.1 We implement reasonable security practices and procedures, including:

(a) Encryption of Health Data **at rest** and **in transit** (TLS 1.2+ / AES-256);

(d) Periodic vulnerability assessments and penetration testing;

10.2 **Breach notification.** In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify you and the **Data Protection Board of India** in accordance with Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act and any rules made thereunder.
